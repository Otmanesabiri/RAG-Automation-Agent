# Customer Support FAQ

## Refunds

Premium customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Standard customers receive a store credit instead of a refund. Refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.

## Shipping

Orders above 50 euros ship for free. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 9.90 euros. International shipping is available to 40 countries.

## Account

Customers can reset their password from the login page using the "Forgot password" link. Accounts inactive for 3 years are deleted after an email warning.

## Error codes

Error code XK-4471 means the payment provider rejected the card. Error code ZB-1020 means the shipping address could not be validated.
